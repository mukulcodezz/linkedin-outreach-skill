# Comment Generator

Commenting on prospects' posts warms leads faster than cold DMs and grows your visibility. Underrated — use it before or alongside DMing.

## Rules for Every Comment
- **1-3 sentences.** Short.
- **Add value** — a real thought, relevant experience, or useful angle. Not "Great post!"
- **Sound human, never AI.** Reference a specific point they made.
- **No pitch. No links. No emojis.**
- Peer-to-peer tone, not fan-to-celebrity.
- Never open with "Great post" / "Well said" / "Couldn't agree more" / "So insightful."

## Comment Types
1. **Add an angle they didn't mention** — extend their point with something new.
2. **Share a relevant micro-experience** — "We've seen the same thing where..."
3. **Ask a genuine question** — "Curious how you handled [X] here?"
4. **Validate + extend (not empty praise)** — affirm one specific point, then build on it.

## What to Generate
Read the post screenshot → output **2 comment options**, each 1-3 sentences, different angle. Then optionally:
> "Good warmup — comment now, DM in a day or two referencing the exchange."

## Tie-in to Outreach
After a comment exchange, the DM opener gets much stronger:
> "Hi [Name], enjoyed the back-and-forth on your post about [topic]..."
Beats any cold opener.

## Run the Tone Checker
Every comment passes `tone_checker.md` (no emojis, no AI-sound, no empty praise).
