# Examples & How-To

## How the skill produces a message
1. Reads the profile screenshot → classifies + scores.
2. Picks opener (config.md §7) and a personalized hook (a real signal from the profile).
3. Default: writes a **nurture** DM (no pitch). Only a Hot lead + strong trigger also gets a **pitch variant**.
4. Runs the tone-checker, then outputs.

```
Hi Priya,

Thanks for connecting.

Saw your post about doubling the team this year — exciting, and usually the point where payroll and compliance start eating real time.

That's exactly what we take off HR's plate at BrightPayroll — payroll that runs itself, no compliance stress. 1,200+ SMBs run on it.

Would you be open to a quick chat to see if it'd help as you scale?
```
*~80-95 words. One pain, one outcome, one proof point, one soft CTA.*

## Good vs bad hook lines
- ❌ "I see you're at a great company doing amazing work." (generic — could be anyone)
- ✅ "Saw your post about opening the Pune office — congrats, big step." (real, specific)
- ❌ "We are the leading provider of..." (ad-speak)
- ✅ "This is usually the part teams underestimate..." (peer insight)
