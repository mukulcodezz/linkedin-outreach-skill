# Follow-Up Sequences (Nurture-First)

Most outreach fails because it pitches too early. This cadence builds a relationship first and mentions your offering LAST, only when relevant. North star: reply rate > sales.

## Cadence
| Touch | When | Goal | Pitch? |
|---|---|---|---|
| DM 1 | Day 0 | Warm opener + one relevance line | NO |
| FU 1 | Day 5-7 | Share a useful insight (config.md §9) | NO |
| FU 2 | Day 14 | Light conversation / engage their content | NO |
| FU 3 | Day 21+ | Mention your offering — only if relevant | SOFT, only if earned |
| Breakup | Day 30+ | Close the loop if still silent | NO |

Stop the moment they reply → `reply_handling.md`.

**Rules:** 30-70 words. Shorter than DM 1. Never repeat a line. No guilt ("noticed you didn't reply"). One soft point each. Run `tone_checker.md` before sending.

## FU 1 (Day 5-7) — Share a useful insight
Give value, ask nothing. Pull from your Value-Add Library (config.md §9).
```
Hi [Name],

Came across something I thought might be useful — [a real, specific insight relevant to their world].

Thought of you given [their role/company]. No agenda, just sharing.
```

## FU 2 (Day 14) — Light conversation
```
Hi [Name],

Saw your recent post on [topic] — [one genuine reaction or short question about it].

Always good to swap notes with people thinking about [their area].
```

## FU 3 (Day 21+) — Mention offering, ONLY if relevant
Only if something they said/posted gives a natural hook. If nothing relevant came up, skip to breakup — don't force it. Phrase as an OUTCOME (config.md §2), never a feature.
```
Hi [Name],

Since you mentioned [the relevant thing], thought I'd flag that this is exactly what we help with at [Company] — [the outcome you deliver].

No pressure at all — happy to share more whenever it's useful.
```

## Breakup (Day 30+) — Close the loop
Often gets the reply. Permission to say no lowers pressure.
```
Hi [Name],

I don't want to keep popping up — I'll leave it here for now.

If [your area] ever lands on your plate, you know where to find me. Wishing you and the team well.
```
Still silent → log `Closed-lost`, stop. Re-approach only if a real trigger appears later.
