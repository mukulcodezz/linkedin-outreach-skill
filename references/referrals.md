# Connector / Referral Play

A prospect who can't buy can still be a **door**. Someone at an ICP-fit company — even a junior or off-function role — can hand you the right person, a company contact, or a warm intro. Treat them as a CONNECTOR.

## When to tag someone a Connector
- Their **employer matches the ICP** (config.md §4 — right size, right industry) BUT
- **Their own role isn't the buyer** (wrong function, too junior, no decision power)

## Score it separately
Print alongside the main score:
```
Connector potential: HIGH | MEDIUM | LOW
```
- **HIGH** — large ICP-fit employer; they can plausibly point to the right person.
- **MEDIUM** — mid-size or partial ICP fit.
- **LOW** — small/off-ICP employer, or no clear path to a buyer.

A Cold direct buyer with HIGH connector potential is still worth a warm relationship.

## The Sequence (never ask cold — kills it)
1. **DM 1 (Day 0):** nurture only. Thanks + genuine relevance. No ask whatsoever.
2. **Day 5-7:** light value / rapport. Still no ask.
3. **Day 14+ (once warm):** soft, low-pressure referral ask with an easy out.

## Referral-Ask Templates (use only once warm)

**Pointer ask:**
```
Hi [Name], quick one — we help companies with [your offering area], and [their company] likely deals with this. Would you happen to know who looks after [function] there, or could you point me the right way? Totally fine if not.
```

**Softer (favor framing):**
```
Hi [Name], hope things are going well. We work with companies on [your area] — [their company] likely has some of this going on. If it's easy, mind nudging me toward whoever handles that side? No worries at all if it's not your area.
```

## Rules
- Always give a clear "no worries if not" out.
- Never make them feel used — keep the relationship genuine regardless.
- One ask. If no response, drop it; stay connected for later.
- If they DO refer → thank them warmly. The referred name is now a **warm, referred lead** — highest priority, treat accordingly.

## Logging
Tag connector leads in `sent_log.md` Status as `Connector`. The goal isn't to sell THEM — it's the intro.

User names ghosted connectors weekly → you remove only those rows from `sent_log.md`. Everything else stays.
