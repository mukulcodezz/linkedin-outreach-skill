# Reply Handling & Objection Framework

When a prospect replies, the goal shifts: **qualify → build trust → move to your closing channel** (config.md §8). Don't over-pitch on LinkedIn — it's slow. Move warm replies to your handoff channel where deals actually move.

## The Golden Rule
Every positive reply has one job: get the conversation to your handoff channel (config.md §8 — WhatsApp/call/email).

## Handoff Line (once they show interest)
Use your template from config.md §8. Pattern:
```
Happy to share specifics — easiest over [channel] if that works for you? I'm on [contact], or share yours and I'll reach out. Whatever's convenient.
```
Never demand. Always give an "or here is fine" out.

## Qualifying Questions (weave in 1-2, don't interrogate)
Adapt to your business. Generic patterns:
- "Roughly how often does [the need] come up for your team?"
- "Anything coming up where this would be relevant?"
- "How are you handling [the area] right now?"
- "Rough size/scope on your side?"
These reveal deal size and urgency.

## Objection Bank (principles + adapt the wording)

### "We already have a vendor / solution"
Don't bash the incumbent. Position as backup/comparison.
> Totally understand — most teams your size do. No pressure to switch. If it's ever useful to have a second option for comparison (or a backup when they're slammed), happy to be on your radar.

### "No budget / not the right time"
Stay warm, plant a seed, set a future trigger.
> Completely fair. When the need comes back around, feel free to ping me. I'll check in down the line if that's alright.

### "Send me details / a proposal"
This is a YES. Get contact + move to your channel.
> Will do. So I tailor it right — [1 qualifying question]? Easiest to send over [channel] — [contact].

### "What does it cost?"
Don't quote blind. Move to a quick conversation.
> Good question — it depends on [scope]. Rather than throw a random number, a quick chat lets me give you something accurate. Free this week? [contact]

### "Who are you / what do you do?"
Crisp 2-line answer (config.md §1 + §2 outcome) + proof + soft ask.
> We're [Company] — [one-line outcome]. [1 proof point]. Happy to share how we'd help [their company] specifically — want a quick overview?

### "Not interested"
Graceful exit, door open, never argue.
> No worries at all, thanks for letting me know. If [your area] ever lands on your plate, you know where to find me.
→ Log `Closed-lost`. Don't follow up again unless a trigger appears later.

### "Interested! / Let's talk"
Move fast before momentum fades.
> Great to hear! Easiest if we hop on [channel] — I can understand your needs and share something tailored quickly. [contact]. Looking forward to it.

## Tone for Replies
Match their energy and length. Adapt to what they actually said — never robotic copy-paste. One clear next step (usually: your channel). Run `tone_checker.md`.

## After a Reply — Log It
Update their row in `sent_log.md`: Status `Replied` → `Booked` / `Closed-won` / `Closed-lost` as it moves. Clear Next Follow-up (stop the sequence).
