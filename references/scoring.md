# Lead Scoring Rubric

Activity-first. An active profile reads and replies; a dead one swallows your message. Roles and tiers come from `config.md` §5.

## Weights
| Factor | Weight | Measure |
|---|---|---|
| **Activity** | 50% | How recently/often they post or engage |
| **Role / Buying Power** | 30% | Can they approve or operate the buying decision? |
| **Fit** | 20% | Does their company match your ICP (config.md §4)? |

## Activity Score (max 5)
| Signal | Points |
|---|---|
| Posted/commented in last 7 days | 5 |
| Last 30 days | 4 |
| Last 3 months | 3 |
| Older than 3 months | 2 |
| No activity visible | 1 |
| Skeleton profile (no photo/posts) | 0.5 |

## Role / Buying Power Score (max 3)
Map their title to a Tier in `config.md` §5:
| Tier | Points |
|---|---|
| Tier A role (owns/operates the decision) | 3 |
| Tier A influencer (executes, recommends) | 2.5 |
| Tier B (high authority, delegates) | 2–2.5 |
| Tier C / unclear | 1 |
| Junior / intern / off-ICP | 0.5 |

> Founder/CEO of a SMALL company = effective buyer → score as Tier A (3). Large-company founder delegates → Tier B (2.5), aim for the warm intro.

## Fit Score (max 2)
| Signal | Points |
|---|---|
| Company clearly matches ICP (size + industry) | 2 |
| Partial fit | 1–1.5 |
| Off-ICP / micro / solopreneur | 0.5 |
| Bonus: industry that obviously needs your offering | +0.5 (cap 2) |

## Trigger Boosts
Add per `references/triggers.md` (cap total at 10). A strong trigger can push Warm → Hot — that's the point.

## Bands
| Score | Band | Action |
|---|---|---|
| 8-10 | **HOT** | Outreach now. Nurture DM; pitch variant allowed if a strong trigger exists. |
| 5-7 | **WARM** | Outreach, medium priority. Nurture only. |
| 1-4 | **COLD** | Low priority. Nurture only, no pitch. |

## Output Format
```
Prospect: [Name] — [Role] (Tier A/B/C)
Score: X/10 — HOT | WARM | COLD
Why: [1 line]
Decision power: YES | MAYBE | NO
Trigger: [name / none]
```
Always print the Tier. Prioritize Tier A.
