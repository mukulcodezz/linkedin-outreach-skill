# Trigger-Based Targeting

The hottest leads are event-driven, not just title-matched. A trigger means something just changed in their world that creates a need or a buying decision. Reading these from the screenshot lifts reply rate sharply. Tailor the "why it matters" to your business in `config.md` §6.

## How Triggers Work
Each trigger spotted adds a **score boost** (see `scoring.md`) AND gives the **best hook line**. Always lead the personalized hook with a trigger if one is visible.

## Tier A Triggers (strongest — +1.5 to +2, reach out now)
- **New job / role change** — new decision-makers rebuild their vendor/tool list in the first 90 days. Window is NOW.
  Hook: "Congratulations on the new role at [Company] — exciting time."
- **Posted about a relevant project / event / initiative** — explicit, live need.
  Hook: "Saw your post about [thing] — that's right in the area we help with."
- **Hiring spree / rapid growth** — scaling creates new needs and budget.
  Hook: "Looks like [Company] is growing fast — usually a lot moving at once."

## Tier B Triggers (+0.5 to +1)
- **Funding round / expansion / new office** — fresh budget, new needs.
- **Promotion** — more budget authority now.
- **Work anniversary** — warm, low-pressure opener.
- **Posted about a pain you solve** — GOLD. They're feeling the exact problem.

## Tier C Triggers (mild — +0.5)
- Industry event/conference season
- Seasonal/holiday relevance to your offering
- Engaging with content in your domain

## No Trigger Visible?
Fall back to a role-based hook (config.md §5 angle). Lower priority but still reach out if activity + role warrant.

## Hook Priority Order
1. Tier A trigger
2. Tier B trigger
3. Recent post topic (shows you read their content)
4. Role-based pain (config.md)
5. Company/industry generic (weakest — avoid if possible)
